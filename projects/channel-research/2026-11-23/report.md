## The edge for the 700 MW film (in one sentence)
Everyone talks about thorium. But the reactor actually carrying India is the one born from a refusal: Canada walked out
of Rawatbhata after Pokhran in 1974, so India built a reactor on its own natural uranium. Its weak point is a few
hundred thin pressure tubes that leaked at Kakrapar in 2015–16. Indian engineers learned to replace every one of them
by remote control inside a radioactive core, and NPCIL now says it can sell that skill abroad.

- Canada ended help on RAPP-2 in 1974 after the test; RAPP-2 started only in 1981 (Bratt, *Nonproliferation Review*).
  Check against a Canadian government or DAE history before use.
- Kakrapar 1 and 2 coolant-channel leaks (2015, 2016): AERB found corrosion from impurities in the CO2 annulus gas,
  limited to those two units; 612 channels replaced, about ₹500 Cr (World Nuclear News; Business Standard).
- RAPS-3 and Kaiga-1: en-masse coolant channel and feeder replacement done "remotely using precision technologies
  developed indigenously" (NPCIL via ThePrint, 2024; Powerline, 17 SEP 2026). "Shortest time" and "export" are
  NPCIL's own claims and must be attributed.
- KAPP-3, the first 700 MW unit: critical 22 JUL 2020; the government told Parliament (2023) there were no design
  flaws. RAPP-8 is the 4th unit; grid power is reportedly expected in 2027 (unconfirmed).
- **Formula fit:** hard machinery under heat, pressure and radiation (roadmap #19); a breakthrough with an honest
  wound; named places (Rawatbhata, Rajasthan; Kakrapar, Gujarat; Kaiga, Karnataka); a memory every Indian shares
  (Pokhran); not an engine; a live date (RAPP-8 to the grid).
- **Objections to answer:** "Nuclear is only 3% of India's power" · "Thorium is the real future, why not that?" ·
  "Kudankulam is Russian" · "Fukushima, safety" · "Pakistan reaction" (ignore the bait).

## Recommendation
The 700 MW reactor film next (a win). The 7 PM grid film after it (civilian, honest bottleneck). Thorium and PFBR go
inside the reactor film as one objection answered, not a film of their own yet. Aridhaman is set aside: no primary
record.
