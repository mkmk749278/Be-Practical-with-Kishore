- 471 subscribers, 149,469 views. Three films made 125,000 of them: Tejas (56,345), rare earths (52,166), API

  part, not a famous thing that is stuck).

  made the films travel; our job is to answer it inside the film.
- **What viewers ask for:** AMCA and its private partner (16 Nov), Tejas Mk1A ("GE delivered 14 engines, how many has
  the IAF accepted?"), Kaveri, thorium reactors, semiconductors / data centres / Indian GPUs.
