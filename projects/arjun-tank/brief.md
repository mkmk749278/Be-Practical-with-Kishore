# Film 4 · Arjun: India's own tank, held up by its engine (working title)

- **Slug:** `arjun-tank` · Drive: `Creator/English/arjun-tank/` (playlist "Practical India | Hard Truths, Simply
  Explained", `PLYqsLgsTPCfg`).
- **Chosen by the owner, 2026-11-17,** after the channel and market research
  (`projects/channel-research/2026-11-17/report.md`): roadmap #8, moved ahead of #4–#7 because it has a household
  name, a stuck number and live news. The towed-array research (#4) is kept for a later navy film.
- **The machine:** the powerpack of a 68-tonne main battle tank: a 1,400 hp German diesel (MTU MB 838 Ka-501) and its
  transmission, which must push 68 tonnes through Thar sand at desert heat; and India's own 1,500 hp DATRAN engine
  that is meant to replace it.
- **The paradox (working):** India began its own tank in 1974. In September 2021 it ordered 118 of the newest
  Arjun Mk1A for Rs 7,523 crore, the first due in 2024. As of 2026 none has been delivered (reported), because the
  German factory stopped making the engine and would restart only for a much bigger order. India built the tank. It
  cannot yet build the heart.
- **Roadmap subtitle check (PLAYBOOK §1.1):** "Kanchan composite vs a 68-tonne tank" holds as a fact (Mk1A about
  68 tonnes, Kanchan armour), but the live bottleneck in 2026 is the engine, not the armour. The weight stays in the
  film as the second problem (bridges, rail, where the tank can go), the engine becomes the spine.
- **Who it's for:** every curious Indian; the people who made "30 Tejas jets are ready, why can't they fly?" our
  biggest film (the same engine story on the ground) and the 3.5M who watched Khan Sir's Arjun video in 2021.
- **Research status (2026-11-17):** first pass in `research/landscape.md`. Primary documents so far: PIB 20 Mar 2024
  (DATRAN maiden test-firing at BEML Mysuru), MoD order 23 Sep 2021 (press), CAG 2017 via The Wire. Still to find:
  a Parliament answer on Mk1A delivery and the engine, the CAG 2017 text, MTU's own statement, the 2010 trial report.
