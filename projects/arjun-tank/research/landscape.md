## 3. Our edge (draft, one sentence)
Everyone argues whether Arjun is a good tank; nobody shows that in 2026 the tank is not the problem: 118 are
ordered and paid for, and they wait for a heart, a 1,400-horsepower diesel a German factory stopped making, while
India's own 1,500-horsepower engine sits on a test bed in Mysuru.

## 8. Second pass (2026-11-17, same session)

### 8.1 Primary documents found
- **CAG Report No. 36 of 2016 (Army Base Workshops, performance audit, tabled 2017), para 3.2.4** (text extracted
  from cag.gov.in): "A total of 124 numbers of Tanks were inducted into the Army from 2004-05 onwards." "At the time of
  induction, 69 per cent components were imported." "owing to the failure of CVRDE in indigenizing the required
  components, HVF could not supply any spares. In the absence of spare support MBT Arjun was not being operational
  since 2013." "MOD in April 2015 directed that DRDO should make 20 tanks with 90% operationally able by August 2015".
  URL: https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2016/Report_36_of_2016_Union_Government_Defence_PA.pdf
- **PIB PRID 2015720 (Mar 2024):** maiden test-firing of the 1,500 HP engine at BEML's engine division, Mysuru,
  Defence Secretary Giridhar Aramane presiding; "high power-to-weight ratio", "operability in extreme conditions
  including high altitudes, sub-zero temperatures, and desert environments"; Generation One (technology
  stabilisation) complete; Generation Two: BEML builds engines for trials at CVRDE. Project initiated August 2020
  (via ThePrint, Smruti Deshpande, 21 Mar 2024). PIB blocks our fetch (403): quote from ThePrint, read PIB on the
  owner's phone or by Cowork. Date: PIB says 20 March; ThePrint's "Tuesday" would be 19 March: settle from PIB.
- **Janes, 21 Feb 2024 (Kapil Kajal):** "deliveries… will experience a delay because of an engine shortage, a DRDO
  official told Janes on 20 February"; MTU did not respond; DRDO to develop an indigenous engine "within three
  years". **Janes, 24 Sep 2021:** per MoD sources, five Mk 1As within 30 months, then 30 a year, complete by about
  2027.
- **Order, 23 Sep 2021:** PIB PRID 1757320 (cited by Drishti), Rs 7,523 crore, HVF Avadi, 72 new features, 200+ MSMEs,
  ~8,000 jobs. Read the PIB text itself before narration.

### 8.3 The objection ledger, with our answers (from sources)
1. *"Only for the desert, too heavy for bridges."* True by design and honest to say: it was built for the Rajasthan
   plains; 68 t against bridges built for about 50 t (The Wire). The film shows where it can and cannot go.
2. *"Why not just buy T-90s?"* India did (about 1,500 T-90S, The Wire); the film says what a T-90 is (48 t, Russian
   design, built under licence) and why the Army still ordered 118 Arjuns: they asked for them in 2021.
3. *"German technology, not Indian."* The CAG's 69% imported at induction, said plainly; then what changed (Mk1A
   "greater indigenous content") and what did not (the engine).
4. *"Rejected by its own Army."* The Army's own order of 118 in 2021 answers it; the 2008 "no further orders"
   (Gen Deepak Kapoor, via The Wire) is the history.
5. *"Same as Tejas: we can't make engines."* The honest answer is the film's spine: not yet, and here is the engine
   that ran in Mysuru, and the date it must pass.
